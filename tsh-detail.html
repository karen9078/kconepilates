About the Temnox Reformer TSH

The Temnox Reformer TSH is a commercial-grade Pilates reformer built for high-traffic studios and professional training environments. Its carbon steel frame carries a 1101 mm carriage travel and a 10-position bar adjustment. Four custom heavy-duty silent pulleys with premium bearings and a 360° silent rope pulley deliver smooth, quiet performance, while KCONE custom imported springs provide consistent resistance. Supplied as standard with a raised jump board, sitting box and double-loop straps.

Finished in Black, as part of the Temnox line. Our 5-spring system provides versatile resistance levels suitable for both beginner and advanced practitioners.

Ideal for: Pilates studios, fitness centers, rehabilitation clinics, and hospitality fitness facilities requiring durable, commercial-grade reformers.
