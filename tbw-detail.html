About the Temnox Wunda Chair TBW

The Temnox Wunda Chair TBW is a professional Pilates chair built with a carbon steel frame. Its foot-pedal axle runs on four independent bearings for zero play and noticeably smoother rotation, while the high-precision pedal linkage keeps every rep consistent. It uses Merrithew-series springs and carries a 3-year warranty for the axle assembly. The compact footprint makes it an excellent space-saving addition to any studio.
