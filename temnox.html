OUR PHILOSOPHY

Seven years of addition.
Then, one decisive subtraction.

Since 2018, KCONE has manufactured professional Pilates equipment. In 2019, we began exploring what a Pilates machine could be — adding function, adding features, adding detail. We did the addition, in our own way.

Until one day we asked ourselves: "When a person begins to practice, what do they truly need?" The answer kept getting simpler. So we did the subtraction.

Reliable Craft

So the body can focus on the feeling of movement.

Intuitive Use

So the practice returns to its essence.

Restrained Design

So the machine is forgotten entirely.
