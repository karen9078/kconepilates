About the Temnox Spine Corrector TBJ

The Temnox Spine Corrector TBJ is a professional Pilates spine corrector wrapped in premium imported leather, combining classic design with modern durability. The upholstery has been fully upgraded — a new generation of premium leather with denser padding, tighter stitching and improved abrasion resistance, for a firmer grip and a longer service life in daily studio use. Every unit is hand-finished by experienced craftsmen: the leather wrap, the stitching and the frame fit are checked piece by piece before the corrector leaves the workshop. The ergonomic carry handle makes it easy to move between stations, ideal for busy studio environments.

This compact yet essential Pilates apparatus is perfect for spinal articulation exercises, chest opening, and posture correction. KCONE offers standard product support including KCONE standard product presentation and export packaging information for distributors and studios worldwide.

Ideal for: Pilates studios, physical therapy clinics, personal training studios, and home studios seeking professional-grade spine correctors with factory-direct quotation support.
