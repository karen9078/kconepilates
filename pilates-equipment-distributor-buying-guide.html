Distributor Buying Guide

How Pilates Equipment Distributors Can Evaluate a New Product Line

A distributor is choosing more than individual machines. The product line must be understandable, supportable and suitable for repeat B2B orders across different studio customers.

Quick Answer

Define the customers first — boutique studios, group spaces, academies and wellness buyers need different product lines.

Look for a coherent range: core training equipment, complementary apparatus and clearly defined specialized categories.

Check information quality before product quality — specs, photos, videos, packed dimensions and consistent terminology.

Confirm repeat-order and support potential, then send an inquiry with your market, categories, quantities and FOB port.

KCONE PILATES · 12 AUGUST 2026

What customers does the product line need to serve?

Before reviewing a supplier catalog, define the customer groups the product line needs to serve. Boutique studios, larger group reformer spaces, instructor academies, wellness facilities and rehabilitation-oriented buyers may ask for different combinations of equipment and information.

A clear customer profile helps a distributor decide whether to prioritize core reformer equipment, complementary apparatus, specialty equipment or a broader studio package. It also keeps sales conversations focused on use rather than on a list of unrelated products.

What makes a product range coherent?

A useful product line should have a clear relationship between its categories. Buyers should be able to understand what each type of equipment is for, how it fits into a studio and what information is needed to compare it.

  • Core training equipment: products that can form the operating base of a studio.
  • Complementary apparatus: chairs, towers, Cadillacs, barrels or other categories that expand the training offer.
  • Specialized categories: products for clearly defined wellness, rehabilitation or education programs.
  • Collection consistency: a visual and professional identity that makes the range easier to present to customers.

Why evaluate information quality before product quality?

A distributor cannot sell or reorder a product confidently when the supporting information is incomplete. Ask the manufacturer for a current specification, product photographs, demonstration videos, included accessory list, packaging information and quotation scope for each product under review.

Also check whether product dimensions are separated from packed dimensions, whether color and configuration options are named consistently, and whether the same terminology is used across the website, quotation and order documents. Clear information reduces avoidable questions later in the sales process.

Product collections can be upgraded. Keep customer-facing materials tied to the current product page and formal quotation instead of treating an old catalog or model list as permanent.

Can the supplier support repeat orders?

Distributors should evaluate the relationship they are building with the supplier. Useful questions include:

  • Can the supplier explain the current collection and recommend categories for different studio formats?
  • Can the supplier provide consistent quotation information for future orders?
  • Are packaging, accessories and order conditions clear enough for the distributor's own sales process?
  • Can the supplier discuss branding requirements or OEM/ODM needs when the project requires them?
  • Is there a practical communication path for product questions, spare parts and after-sales follow-up?

These questions complement KCONE's manufacturer question checklist and help a distributor prepare a more complete first inquiry.

What quotation and handover terms should you confirm?

A distributor's internal margin and customer quotation depend on a clear purchasing scope. Confirm the product category, current configuration, quantity, packaging method, order conditions and production schedule with the manufacturer.

KCONE quotes on FOB Shanghai or DDU terms. Under FOB, the buyer arranges the main international freight with its own forwarder after port handover; under DDU, KCONE arranges delivery to the buyer's destination and the buyer handles import duties and local clearance. Include the preferred trade term in the quotation request and keep the final quotation with the approved product information.

For a broader pre-order framework, see the professional Pilates equipment buying guide.

What should a distributor inquiry include?

A first inquiry is easier to answer when it includes the distributor's market, customer profile, target categories, estimated quantities, preferred configuration, branding needs, timeline and FOB Shanghai or DDU preference.

The goal is not to request every possible product at once. Start with the categories that fit the customers you already serve, then expand the range after the supplier confirms the current collection and project conditions.

Frequently asked questions

What should distributors check before adding a product line?

Check that the range is coherent, the supporting information is current and complete, repeat orders are practical, and quotation, packaging and handover terms fit your own sales process.

Why do distributors need current product information?

Customer-facing materials such as catalogs and quotations must match what is actually available. Product collections can be upgraded, so tie your materials to the current product page and formal quotation.

What terms should a distributor confirm in the quotation?

Product category, current configuration, quantity, packaging method, order conditions, production schedule and the FOB Shanghai or DDU handover port.
