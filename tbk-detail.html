About the Temnox Cadillac TBK

The Temnox Cadillac TBK is a premium Pilates Cadillac / Trapeze Table built on a 3.0 mm-wall 304 stainless steel frame with a 655 mm bed height and a 2229 mm overall height. Recessed stainless steel upright tubes and four one-piece aluminium legs give it the rigidity commercial studios need, and it is supplied with KCONE custom imported springs or Merrithew-size Cadillac springs. At 95 kg it is built for maximum stability and longevity.

At 95 kg, this Cadillac is built for commercial environments requiring maximum stability and longevity. KCONE provides standard product support including standard frame colors, KCONE brand presentation and packaging. The KCONE custom imported springs or Merrithew-size Cadillac springs ensure consistent performance and exceptional lifespan even in high-usage settings.

Ideal for: flagship Pilates studios, rehabilitation centers, university sports programs, and luxury wellness facilities seeking the highest quality Cadillac equipment.

KCONE checks key structures, springs, upholstery, moving parts, accessories, surface finish, and export packaging before shipment. The goal is to keep each KCONE branded product consistent for professional studio use.
