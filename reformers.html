Frequently asked questions

Do you offer sample units or trial orders?

Large Pilates machines are usually evaluated through specifications, photos, videos, packaging details, lead time and quotation discussions rather than small-sample purchasing. KCONE can arrange video calls and detailed product reviews before order confirmation.

What is the MOQ for reformers?

MOQ is flexible and depends on the model, configuration and destination. Include your target quantity in the inquiry and KCONE will confirm the current MOQ with the quotation.

How are reformers packed for export?

Reformers are protected with professional wooden crate or carton packaging. KCONE quotes on FOB Shanghai or DDU; buyers arrange the main international freight with their own forwarder.

What is the production lead time?

Lead time depends on the selected configuration, order quantity and current production plan. Confirm the current schedule with your quotation request.
