FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About
KCONE Equipment

Does KCONE offer OEM and private label Pilates equipment?

Yes. KCONE offers full OEM and ODM services including custom colors, logo branding, packaging, and structural modifications for distributors, chain studios, and fitness brands.

What Pilates equipment does KCONE manufacture?

KCONE manufactures a full range of professional Pilates equipment including reformers (H02, KC01, M01), Cadillac (K21), Wunda Chair (W01), Ladder Barrel (T02), Tower (H21), Spine Corrector (J01), and the new PowerHouse RX upright trainer.

Does KCONE ship Pilates equipment internationally?

Yes. KCONE ships globally under FOB trade terms with professional wooden crate packaging. We have served studios and distributors worldwide since 2018.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale Pilates equipment?

KCONE offers flexible MOQ to accommodate both startup studios and large chain operations. Contact us directly for specific MOQ details, lead times, and wholesale pricing.

How long has KCONE been manufacturing Pilates equipment?

KCONE (originally founded as INIKO) has been manufacturing professional Pilates equipment since 2018. In 2025, the brand underwent a full upgrade to the KCONE identity, with an expanded product line and partnership with PHI Pilates for joint R&D and instructor training.
