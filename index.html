FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About
KCONE Equipment

What is the design philosophy behind KCONE products?

We specialize in proprietary brand design, reflecting true craftsmanship in every detail. From human ergonomics to material selection, we spare no effort to deliver outstanding quality that withstands the test of time.

What Pilates equipment does KCONE manufacture?

KCONE manufactures professional Pilates equipment across reformer, Wunda Chair, Cadillac, Tower, Ladder Barrel, Spine Corrector and specialized training categories. The published collection may be updated as product development progresses, so buyers should confirm the current product page and quotation.

Does KCONE ship Pilates equipment internationally?

KCONE quotes on FOB Shanghai or DDU terms with professional wooden crate export packaging. Under FOB, the buyer arranges the main international freight with its own forwarder after port handover; under DDU, KCONE arranges delivery to the buyer's destination and the buyer handles import duties and local clearance.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale Pilates equipment?

KCONE offers flexible MOQ to accommodate both startup studios and large chain operations. Buyers can review specifications, photos, videos, FOB export packaging details, lead time, and quotation information before order confirmation.

How can buyers evaluate KCONE equipment before ordering?

Buyers can review KCONE product specifications, detailed photos, demonstration videos, FOB export packaging details, lead time, and quotation information before order confirmation.

What quality checks does KCONE complete before shipment?

KCONE checks key structures, springs, upholstery, moving parts, accessories, surface finish, and export packaging before shipment. The goal is to keep every KCONE branded product consistent for professional studio use.

How long has KCONE been manufacturing Pilates equipment?

KCONE (originally founded as INIKO) has been manufacturing professional Pilates equipment since 2018. In 2025, the brand underwent a full upgrade to the KCONE identity, with an expanded product line and partnership with PHI Pilates for joint R&D and instructor training.

Can KCONE help with a full Pilates studio equipment setup?

Yes. KCONE can support full studio equipment planning with reformers, Cadillacs, Wunda Chairs, ladder barrels, towers, spine correctors, and PowerHouse RX units. Buyers can share studio size, training style, target capacity, and budget range for a more accurate equipment list and quotation.

How does KCONE ensure consistent product quality?

KCONE controls quality through proprietary product design, selected materials, production inspection, tested spring systems, laser-welded structures, protective export packaging, and final checks before shipment. Every model is built as part of the KCONE branded equipment collection.

What information should I provide to get an accurate quotation?

For a faster quotation, please provide the product models, estimated quantity, FOB Shanghai or DDU preference, preferred timeline, studio or distributor role, and any packaging information requirements. Photos or floor plans can also help when planning a full studio package.

How does KCONE support buyers after delivery?

KCONE provides after-sales support through WhatsApp, email, and WeChat. Support may include setup guidance, maintenance suggestions, spare parts coordination, and follow-up communication for studios, distributors, and chain buyers.
